Route your own boat

Not in the list above? If your boat holds an ORR or ORR-Ez certificate, you can upload it and route on its exact polars.

  1. Open the current ORR certificate list ↗ on regattaman.com.
  2. Find your boat and click its Cert ID.
  3. On the certificate page, scroll to the bottom and click Print.
  4. In the print dialog, choose Save as PDF and save the file.
  5. Back here, click ＋ ORR cert and pick that PDF.

Your boat is added and selected automatically — then choose a start point or hit Route from my location. (We read the page-2 boat-speed polar table.)
